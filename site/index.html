What it does

Survival odds

400 simulated drafts per pick: the chance your guy is still there at your next turn, so you know when you can wait and when waiting loses him.

The live board

Every pick mirrored in seconds, plus four shortlists of the best available by position — ordered by your strategy, with value and reach flagged against ADP.

Plain English

"Olave or a third RB?" A short answer while you're on the clock, the full reasoning when you ask for more. Injuries and rookie context are priced in.

Built on real draft data

Rankings from Joel Smyth's 2026 Draft Guide, live market ADP, and the picks happening in your room.

Questions, answered honestly

Does it make picks for me?
No, and it can't. There is no code path that submits a pick, clicks a button in your draft room, or automates your account. It advises; you draft.
Does it see my password?
Never. Sleeper drafts are read through Sleeper's public API — no login involved. Yahoo uses Yahoo's own official OAuth sign-in page; this service receives a read-only token you can revoke in your Yahoo settings at any time. Google sign-in works the same way — Google verifies you and this service never sees a password.
What does it cost?
It's free. If hosting costs ever change that, the change will be announced here first — no surprise paywalls mid-season.
Is my draft data stored?
Without an account, your session — the draft you connect and your chat — is anonymous and expires automatically after inactivity. With an account, your drafts are archived under it until you delete them. Details in the privacy policy.
Can I self-host it?
Yes — the whole thing is open source at https://github.com/SidSub1041/ff-draft-bot, and runs locally with one command. The hosted service is the same code.
Where do the rankings come from?
The engine's value model is fit to Joel Smyth's 2026 Draft Guide — rankings, adjusted points-per-game tables, and draft strategy — combined with live market ADP, injury data, and Monte Carlo simulation. He doesn't endorse this project; we just think his work is excellent.
