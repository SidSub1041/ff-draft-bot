How it advises

An engine with receipts, not vibes

Every suggestion is a scored breakdown you can expand: projection versus positional replacement, ADP value or reach, tier scarcity, roster fit, injury status, and the simulated odds at your next pick. Rankings and strategy are built on Joel Smyth's 2026 Draft Guide, and the engine shows its work on every pick. It recommends — the pick is always yours.

Questions, answered honestly

Does it make picks for me?
No, and it can't. There is no code path that submits a pick, clicks a button in your draft room, or automates your account. It advises; you draft.
Does it see my password?
Never. Sleeper drafts are read through Sleeper's public API — no login involved. Yahoo uses Yahoo's own official OAuth sign-in page; this service receives a read-only token you can revoke in your Yahoo settings at any time.
What does it cost?
It's free. If hosting costs ever change that, the change will be announced here first — no surprise paywalls mid-season.
Is my draft data stored?
Your session — the draft you connect and your chat — is anonymous and expires automatically after inactivity. No accounts, no emails collected. Details in the privacy policy.
Can I self-host it?
Yes — the whole thing is open source at GITHUB_LINK, and runs locally with one command. The hosted service is the same code.
Where do the rankings come from?
The engine's value model is fit to Joel Smyth's 2026 Draft Guide — rankings, adjusted points-per-game tables, and draft strategy — combined with live market ADP, injury data, and Monte Carlo simulation. He doesn't endorse this project; we just think his work is excellent.
