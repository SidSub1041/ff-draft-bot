How it works

Three steps, no setup ritual.

1

Install the extension

Add Draft Copilot to Chrome. Or skip the extension and use the web app in a second window.

2

Open your draft

Head to your Sleeper or Yahoo Fantasy draft room like you normally would.

3

The copilot docks beside it

A panel appears next to your draft board with live rankings, odds, and a chat box. That's it.

What it actually does

Real features, nothing padded.

Live draft board mirror

Every pick shows up in the panel the moment it happens. No refreshing, no retyping picks into a spreadsheet.

Best available, by position

Not a static cheat sheet. Rankings re-sort as the draft unfolds, weighted by your strategy and roster needs.

"Will he last?" survival odds

Monte Carlo simulations estimate the chance a player is still on the board at your next pick, so you know when to reach and when to wait.

Plain-English assistant

Ask it things like "who should I take here" or "compare these two backs" and get a straight answer with the reasoning behind it.

Injury flags & rookie logic

Injury designations surface right on the board, and rookies are weighted by real NFL draft capital, not preseason hype.

Strategy presets, tunable mid-draft

RB-heavy, zero-RB, hero-RB, BPA, superflex, dynasty. Pick one to start, then adjust it on the fly when the draft goes sideways.

How it advises

No black box, no magic. Here's what's under the hood.

The numbers. The engine starts from rankings built on the Joel Smyth 2026 Draft Guide — genuinely great work, go read it — then layers on live context: positional scarcity as the board thins, your roster's actual holes, injury flags, rookie draft capital, and Monte Carlo survival odds for your upcoming picks.

The strategy. Your chosen preset shapes how those numbers are weighed. Zero-RB and RB-heavy see the same board very differently, and you can retune the strategy mid-draft when your plan meets reality.

Advisory only, always. Draft Copilot never makes a pick for you, never clicks anything in your draft room, and never will. It puts its best case in front of you — the final call is yours. If you fade the bot and it works out, you get the credit.

FAQ

Does it make picks for me?

No. Never. It recommends, ranks, and explains — you click the pick button in your draft room yourself. There is no auto-draft mode and we won't build one.

Does it see my password?

No. Sleeper requires no login at all — the copilot reads the live draft without credentials. Yahoo Fantasy connects through Yahoo's official OAuth flow, which means you sign in on Yahoo's site and Yahoo hands us a limited token. Your password never touches our servers.

What does it cost?

Nothing right now — everything is free while we're in beta. If paid tiers show up later, we'll say so plainly and ahead of time. No surprise paywalls mid-draft.

Is my draft data stored?

Draft sessions are temporary and expire after your draft ends. We don't build a profile on you or keep your league data around. Details in the privacy policy.

Can I self-host it?

Yes. The project is open source — grab it at GITHUB_LINK and run the whole thing on your own machine.

What about ESPN or NFL.com leagues?

Not supported yet. Both are on the list for later, but we're not going to promise a date we can't keep.
